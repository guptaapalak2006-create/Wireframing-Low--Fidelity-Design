Design rationale

Palette: Paper (#FAF3E7) and Ink (#241B15) form a warm, high-contrast base so food photography stays the most saturated thing on screen. Chili (#E4572E) is reserved strictly for primary actions and selected states — never used decoratively — so it keeps its meaning as "tap here." Turmeric flags secondary highlights (ratings, badges) and Basil is reserved for positive/confirmation states (promo applied, delivered), giving the app a simple 3-color status language.

Typography: Archivo Black gives headlines a bold, menu-board character without leaning on an overused serif-and-terracotta look. Work Sans keeps body copy and UI labels highly legible at small mobile sizes. Space Mono — used only for prices and the order-ticket — borrows the fixed-width feel of a receipt printer, making cost figures instantly scannable and tying the checkout flow visually to a real paper ticket.

Consistency & accessibility: corner radii, shadow depth, icon stroke-weight, and the ink/chili "selected" logic repeat identically across all 7 screens; every tap target is 44px or larger; text-on-paper and text-on-white contrast pass WCAG AA; status is always communicated with both color and text (never color alone).
